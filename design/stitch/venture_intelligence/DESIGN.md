## Brand & Style

The design system delivers an institutional-grade, hyper-lucid aesthetic engineered for high-velocity venture capital deal flow and portfolio intelligence. Drawing inspiration from pristine fintech architecture and dense developer tools, it marries extreme high-contrast readability with razor-sharp editorial restraint. 

The personality is analytical, authoritative, and frictionless. It projects total financial transparency through stark contrast: brilliant white canvases anchored by deep obsidian structures, animated exclusively by fresh pastel mint performance badges and airy sky-blue indicators. 

Visual principles:
- **Surgical Precision:** Strict visual hierarchy, low visual noise, and intentional hairline boundaries.
- **Data Densification with Dignity:** High data density rendered effortlessly readable via disciplined spatial pacing and micro-typography.
- **Restrained Vitality:** Color is strictly transactional—employed only to communicate status, performance trajectory, and critical actionable signals.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-contrast paradigm rooted in pure monochromatic surfaces, accented exclusively by clean pastel-tinted status containers and dark obsidian structural text. Purple, indigo, and deep royal hues are strictly prohibited.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F8FAFC` (Secondary card fills, table headers)
  - Surface Neutral: `#F1F5F9` (Nested panel states, tag backgrounds)
  - Surface Border: `#E2E8F0` (Hairline crisp card borders and dividers)
  - Border Subdued: `#F1F5F9` (Subtle internal table row borders)

- **Typography & Structure (Obsidian Core):**
  - Primary Text: `#09090B` (High-contrast obsidian for headlines, metrics, primary copy)
  - Secondary Text: `#52525B` (Supporting metadata, captions, inactive labels)
  - Tertiary / Muted Text: `#A1A1AA` (Placeholder copy, disabled states)

- **Performance & Signals (Fresh Mint Green):**
  - Accent Solid: `#16A34A` (Accessible high-contrast text, primary action anchors)
  - Active Fill: `#4ADE80` (Charts, high-momentum sparklines)
  - Soft Tint Fill: `#DCFCE7` (Performance badges, valuation increase chips)
  - Border Tint: `#86EFAC` (Chip boundaries, signal container borders)

- **Intelligence & Allocation (Soft Baby Blue):**
  - Accent Solid: `#0284C7` (Interactive links, active navigation, focus rings)
  - Active Fill: `#38BDF8` (Allocation charts, secondary metric indicators)
  - Soft Tint Fill: `#F0F9FF` (Deal stage tags, syndicate chips)
  - Border Tint: `#BAE6FD` (Informational pill borders)

## Typography

Typography establishes an unwavering baseline of institutional authority. Headlines in Hanken Grotesk provide modern, crisp geometry with tightly tucked tracking, while body copy in Inter delivers optimal micro-legibility for deal memo scanning, term sheets, and complex numeric tabular data.

### Typographic Guidelines
- All numbers, valuations, multiples (e.g., 3.4x), and percentage changes should leverage tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across data grids.
- Category identifiers, column heads, and status indicators strictly leverage `label-sm` with slight positive tracking (`0.02em`) and uppercase formatting where appropriate.
- Hero metrics utilize `metric-lg` with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) to anchor financial cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured 12-column grid system designed for data density on desktop displays, scaling gracefully down to modular single-column views on mobile devices.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 2rem canvas margins, 1.5rem gutters. Data tables and pipeline boards span all 12 columns, while deal memos and company dossiers occupy an 8/4 split (primary details / deal telemetry).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 1.5rem gutters, collapsible side navigation.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, 1rem margins, 1rem gutters. Sticky tabular horizontally-scrolling metrics.
- **Density:** Financial tables maintain a dense 40px row height for high visual ingestion, expanding to 56px rows only when interactive avatars or stage flows are present.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through crisp, surgical boundaries rather than heavy skeuomorphism or deep atmospheric shadows. The system uses a disciplined light-touch paradigm:

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#FFFFFF` pure white background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Panels):** `#FFFFFF` surfaced over subtle hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0` coupled with an ultra-diffused micro shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered States & Active Modals):** Hairline border transitions to `#CBD5E1` with elevated depth: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Menus & Tooltips):** Obsidian floating elements with `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.04)` over a `1px solid #09090B` or crisp white elevated surfaces with `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

No heavy colored or neon drop shadows are allowed. All depth must feel crisp, optical, and pristine.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, precision-engineered shape language (Roundedness Level 1). UI elements stay tailored and architectural, avoiding generic hyper-rounded bubbles or sharp brutalist edges.

### Token Mapping
- **Default Base (`rounded`):** `0.25rem` (4px). Applied to badges, table cell pills, data inputs, segmented control segments, and micro-buttons.
- **Panels & Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Applied to financial deal cards, stage pipeline containers, modal dialogs, and slide-overs.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Reserved for top-level analytical dashboards, embedded charts, and canvas wrappers.
- **Status Indicators:** Full circular `9999px` strictly for user avatars and circular presence pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Obsidian `#09090B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius, `0.875rem` font size, `500` weight. Hover: `#18181B`. Active: subtle transform compression `scale(0.99)`.
- **Secondary / Actionable Neutral:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `#09090B` text. Hover: `#F8FAFC`, border color `#CBD5E1`.
- **Primary Positive (Commit / Deploy Capital):** `#16A34A` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover: `#15803D`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#52525B` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9`, `#09090B` text.

### Performance Chips & Status Badges
- **Momentum / Up-Round (Green):** Background `#DCFCE7`, border `1px solid #86EFAC`, text `#16A34A`. Typographic weight `600`, size `0.75rem`. Includes a mini upward delta glyph.
- **Stage / Informational (Blue):** Background `#F0F9FF`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`, text `#0284C7`. Used for "Series A", "Term Sheet Issued", and "Lead Syndicate".
- **Neutral / Draft (Obsidian):** Background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#52525B`.

### Cards & Pipeline Boards
- Pristine white `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `8px` corner radius.
- Internal card padding: `1.25rem`.
- Card Header: Flex row containing title (`headline-sm`), deal stage pill, and right-aligned numerical metric in tabular figures (`metric-lg`).
- Divider lines inside cards: `1px solid #F1F5F9`.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Table Header: `#F8FAFC` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` bottom border, `label-sm` uppercase text in `#52525B`.
- Row Height: `44px` compact. Hover state transitions cell background to `#F8FAFC`.
- Right-aligned numeric metrics (IRR, MoIC, ARR, Round Size). Left-aligned company entities.

### Inputs & Filters
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `4px`, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`.
- Focus state: Border color `#0284C7`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #0284C7`. No heavy offset glows.
- Search inputs include monochromatic `#A1A1AA` magnifying glass glyph and keyboard shortcut cue (`⌘K`) in a `rounded` `#F1F5F9` container.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Radios
- Size: `16px x 16px`, `4px` corner radius.
- Unchecked: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked: `#09090B` fill with `#FFFFFF` hairline checkmark icon.
- Toggles: `#E2E8F0` track, transitioning to `#16A34A` when activated; `#FFFFFF` thumb with subtle elevation.
